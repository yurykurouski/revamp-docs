Revamp SaaS API (2.1.0)

Download OpenAPI specification:

URL: https://github.com/yurykurouski/revamp-dev License: Proprietary (diploma project)

Revamp finds local businesses with dated websites, audits each site in a real browser, has a model design a new home page from the site's own content, and sends the owner a personal email with the prototype only after an operator approves it (Human-in-the-Loop).

This specification describes the REST API in apps/api (Express 4, TypeScript) of the Revamp-dev monorepo. Long work (audits, generation, publishing, email) runs in BullMQ workers; the API stores the request, queues a job and answers at once (202), and the client polls the resource.

  • Base URL: /api/v1 (API_PREFIX). Locally http://localhost:4000/api/v1.
  • Authentication: Authorization: Bearer <JWT> from POST /auth/register or POST /auth/login (HS256, 7 days, no refresh token). Routes marked public need no token. See guides/authentication.md.
  • Studios (tenants): every lead, MVP, campaign and event belongs to the caller's studio. A record of another studio answers the same 404 as a missing one.
  • Roles: owner (everything, plus members), operator (reads and writes), viewer (reads only; every non-GET request answers 403 FORBIDDEN).
  • Success envelope: { "success": true, "data": … }, sometimes with message, pagination or total.
  • Error envelope: { "success": false, "error": { "code", "message", "details?" } }; branch on code. The codes are listed in ApiErrorCode and explained in guides/errors.md.
  • Request validation: every body and query passes a Zod schema of @revamp/validation before any work. Request schemas in schemas/requests.yaml are generated from those Zod schemas.

Leads

Businesses the studio pursues and their pipeline status

Add a lead and queue its audit

Finds or creates the shared business of the site. If the business already has a completed audit, the lead is created AUDITED with that audit and MVP generation is queued (message says so). Otherwise the lead and its first audit (QUEUED) are written in one transaction and the audit job is queued.

If the queue does not accept the job within 5 s, the lead is moved to AUDIT_FAILED and the answer is 503 AUDIT_QUEUE_UNAVAILABLE with details.leadId; retry with POST /audits/trigger. Without contactEmail the audit takes the email from the site; none is invented.

Adding a lead claims the studio's exclusive right to contact the business (REV-225) in the same transaction: CONTACT_RIGHT_DAYS (14 days), then a cooldown of CONTACT_COOLDOWN_DAYS (30 days) for every studio. A business another studio holds, in cooldown or opted out is refused with a 409 and nothing is created. A refusal never names the other studio.

Allowed for a lead in QUEUED or AUDIT_FAILED; the lead goes back to QUEUED, its auditError is cleared and a new Audit document is created. A lead further along answers 409 LEAD_NOT_AUDITABLE with details.status: an audit never moves a lead backwards.

Approve the email (the Human-in-the-Loop gate)

The only way an email to a business is ever sent. Allowed only from NEEDS_APPROVAL. The lead moves to SCHEDULED and its EmailCampaign is written in one MongoDB transaction filtered on the status, so two approvals at once cannot both queue an email. The send is queued with a random delay of 15–45 s.
